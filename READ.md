### 🤖 Featured Project: AI Career Scout
The crown jewel of this portfolio is the **AI Career Scout**, an intelligent, agentic workspace embedded directly within the platform.
* **Real-Time Streaming**: Utilizing direct asynchronous streams to Google's Gemini models, it delivers lightning-fast, real-time career mapping data.
* **Context-Aware Analysis**: Built to ingest career history, analyze enterprise role requirements, and map out gap-analysis vectors instantly.
